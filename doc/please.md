### Looking for testers for my Android app — happy to test yours in return

Hi everyone!

I've just started a **closed test** for my Android app **Cutting Planner** and I'm looking for a few developers who could give it a try.

**Cutting Planner** is a simple tool for furniture makers and woodworkers to create sheet-material cutting layouts. It supports automatic and manual cutting, edgings, offcuts, and exporting layouts to PDF.

I'd especially appreciate feedback on:

* bugs or unexpected results;
* the automatic cutting algorithm;
* usability and confusing parts of the UI;
* anything that doesn't work as expected on your device.
